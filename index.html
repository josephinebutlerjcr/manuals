Manuals

Hello, and welcome to the manuals of the Butler JCR Website and Intranet. Apologies for the design - we did not expect many people to even use this manual, and we wanted this to be as accessible as possible, hence basic.

Webmasters are listed here, so if you are stuck, you know who to contact within the university. Alternatively, if you are a new webmaster, and very stuck, you can contact me (Prem) by clicking on the hyperlink and going to my website - finding my LinkedIn / email.

The purpose of this manual is to help general users, exec members, and the webmaster deal with this.

To the webmaster (and indeed anyone else helping on the website) - this manual does also include details on the technical implementations, but this manual does NOT include any details on the server. This is in your handover document.
