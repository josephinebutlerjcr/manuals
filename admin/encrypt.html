Encrypt: College Families

Here, please do it so that we have on each line: CIS of college child, CIS of parent 1, CIS of parent 2 (optional).
For example, if our child is `chil00`, and our two parents are `pare01` and `pare02` (CIS codes), the line for this child will be `chil00,pare01,pare02` (or `chil00,pare02,pare01` works too).
If you have it as a CSV file, you could have it so the first column is the child, and the next 2 are of parents. You can save it as a CSV, close it, and instead of opening it, press right click, and `Open as Text` or `Open with Notebook` or `Open with...` and select Notebook. Copy and paste what is in there - it should be in the right format.
